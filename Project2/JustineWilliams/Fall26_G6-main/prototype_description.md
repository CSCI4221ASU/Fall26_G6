# CampusConnect Prototype Description

**Prepared by:** Group 6

CampusConnect is an application designed to help college students find important campus information in one place. The prototype was created in Figma and includes connected screens and working navigation buttons.

## Prototype Screens

1. **Login and Registration** – Allows students to create an account or sign into an existing account.
2. **Home Screen** – Gives students access to the main features of CampusConnect.
3. **Campus Events** – Displays upcoming events taking place on campus.
4. **Event Details** – Provides additional information about a selected event.
5. **Student Organizations** – Helps students learn about organizations available on campus.
6. **Academic Resources** – Provides information about tutoring, workshops, and other academic support.
7. **Notifications** – Alerts students about upcoming events and important updates.
8. **Student Profile** – Displays and allows students to manage their account information.

## Prototype Testing

The team tested the prototype’s screens, buttons, and navigation. We reviewed the prototype from beginning to end and confirmed that the required features were included and functioning correctly.
