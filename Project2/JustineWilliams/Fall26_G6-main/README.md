#### Product Vision Statement

     Our product idea is called CampusConnect. CampusConnect is a platform designed to help college students stay informed about campus events, student organizations, academic resources, and other opportunities. The goal of the product is to create one convenient place where students can discover what is happening on campus and become more involved in the university community.
