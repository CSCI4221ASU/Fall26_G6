# CampusConnect User Types and Usage

**Prepared by:** Justin Williams

CampusConnect is designed for different members of the college community. Each type of user will use the app in a different way based on what they need.

## Students

Students are the main users of CampusConnect. They can create an account, build a student profile, search for campus events, learn about student organizations, find academic resources, and receive notifications about upcoming events.

## Student Organization Leaders

Organization leaders can use CampusConnect to share information about their organizations and promote upcoming meetings or events. This will make it easier for students to discover organizations and become involved on campus.

## Faculty and Staff

Faculty and staff members can use the app to share academic resources, campus programs, tutoring information, workshops, and other opportunities that may help students.

## Campus Administrators

Campus administrators can help manage the information displayed in the app. They can review campus events, organization information, academic resources, and notifications to make sure the content is accurate and appropriate.

## Overall Usage

The purpose of CampusConnect is to place useful campus information in one location. Instead of searching through different websites, emails, and social media pages, students can use the app to stay informed and connected with their campus community.
