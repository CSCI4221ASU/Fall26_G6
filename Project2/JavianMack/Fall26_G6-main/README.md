## Project 1

### Product Vision Statement

Our product idea is called CampusConnect. CampusConnect is a platform designed to help college students stay informed about campus events, student organizations, academic resources, and other opportunities. The goal of the product is to create one convenient place where students can discover what is happening on campus and become more involved in the university community.

### Team Meeting Minutes

During our group meeting, Group 6 discussed possible ideas for our project and the responsibilities of each team member. We agreed that communication and teamwork will be important throughout the development of the project.

The team discussed the CampusConnect idea and identified some of its potential features. These features include student accounts, campus event listings, organization information, academic resources, and notifications for upcoming events.

Tasks were divided among group members based on different roles. Each member will be responsible for completing their assigned work and communicating their progress with the rest of the team.
